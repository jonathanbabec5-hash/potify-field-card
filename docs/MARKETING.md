# Field Card 01 marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Field-note keepers and zine readers. **Offer.** Give a grower a practical field card they can open, fold and use away from the screen.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Show a real fold sequence, then one blank space a grower can use during an observation. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Download and fold the free card. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 10 confirmed downloads. |

### Sales test

Offer a three-card physical set only after a signed-off printer proof. Collect three price-confirmed requests and price from a real print and postage quote. Test a printed card pack after three readers request physical copies. Count orders after fulfillment.

### Measurement and decision

Use `field-card-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** Local download verification establishes the file only. Original remote reporting depends on a separate worker. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
