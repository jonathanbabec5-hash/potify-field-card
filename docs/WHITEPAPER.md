# Field Card 01
## Whitepaper · public developer edition

**November 25, 2026 · Printable publication and reading pages**

### Goal and audience

Give a grower a practical field card they can open, fold and use away from the screen. The immediate audience is field-note keepers and zine readers.

### Design and implementation

The original PDF and two reading pages carry the field observations and instructions. The local edition provides a direct PDF path without a reporting service. The card needs to work for someone with a printer and a folded sheet, including when JavaScript is disabled. Keep the printable PDF and the companions together.

### What a contributor can establish

The public entry page returned HTTP 200 and rendered local content in Chromium at a 390 × 844 viewport. A download proves the file was delivered. A legible two-sided test print is a stronger product acceptance check.

### Evidence and outstanding work

Local download verification establishes the file only. Original remote reporting depends on a separate worker. The next acceptance case is concrete. A reader can open the PDF and produce a legible two-sided test print.

### Place in POTIFY

Test a printed card pack after three readers request physical copies. Count orders after fulfillment. The marketing plan gives the test window and the evidence for a commercial outcome.
